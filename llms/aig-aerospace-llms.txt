# AIG Aerospace

> AIG Aerospace is the aviation and aerospace specialty insurance business of American International
> Group, Inc. (AIG). It underwrites hull and liability cover for airlines, general aviation owners and
> operators, aircraft and component manufacturers, MRO and distribution businesses, airports, ground
> handlers and service providers, and unmanned aircraft systems, with dedicated aerospace adjusters
> and aviation attorneys on a 24/7 claims hotline.

> Generated by API Evangelist on 2026-09-14 from the AIG Aerospace public surface. AIG does not
> publish an llms.txt; /llms.txt returns 404 on www.aig.com, www.aig.co.uk and www-249.aig.com.

## No API surface

AIG Aerospace has **no public API**. There is no developer portal, no OpenAPI or other
machine-readable contract, no GraphQL endpoint, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no SDK, no CLI, no
webhooks, no sandbox and no self-service signup. Every /.well-known/ discovery path probed on every
AIG host missed. Quoting and binding run through appointed brokers against PDF application forms;
claims run through authenticated AIG portals.

The parent company operates one API gateway, `commercial.api.aig.com`, which serves AIG's broker and
producer applications, rejects anonymous calls at an Okta-backed proxy, and publishes no contract.
It is not an aerospace surface and is profiled separately at https://apis.io/providers/aig/.

An agent asked to integrate with AIG Aerospace should route the request to a human: an appointed
aviation broker for placement, or the aerospace claims hotline for a loss.

## Products

- [Aviation & Aerospace Insurance](https://www.aig.com/home/risk-solutions/business/specialty-risks/aviation): Line overview, capacity and application forms.
- [Airline Insurance](https://www.aig.com/home/risk-solutions/business/specialty-risks/aerospace-and-aviation/airline): Hull and liability for scheduled charter through international wide-body operators; up to 15% of a $2.5B limit of liability.
- [Unmanned Aircraft Systems Insurance](https://www.aig.com/home/risk-solutions/business/specialty-risks/aerospace-and-aviation/unmanned-aircraft-systems): Physical damage and third-party liability for drone and UAS exposures.
- [AIG Aviation (UK)](https://www.aig.co.uk/home/risk-solutions/business/aviation): The UK arm of the same line.
- General aviation: hull and liability for owners, operators and aircraft management companies, up to $100M agreed hull value and $650M liability limits.
- Aviation product liability: manufacturers, distributors, and maintenance, repair and overhaul operations.
- Airports, ground handlers and aviation service providers liability.

## Claims

- [Report an Aerospace Claim](https://www.aig.com/home/claims/report-a-claim/specialty-aerospace): 24/7 hotline 866-463-0408 / 404-249-1800; intake mailboxes AerospaceClaimsPhoenix@aig.com (standard aerospace) and AerospaceClaimsHomeOffice@aig.com (airline, products, environmental, airport).
- [AIG Claims Portal](https://www-249.aig.com/): Authenticated portal for reporting aerospace and other commercial claims. Login required.
- AIG Claims Promise: 50% of AIG's share advanced within 7 days of confirmed coverage and ownership.
